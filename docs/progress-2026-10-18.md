# Overnight results, 18 October 2026

What the runs that finished between 21:30 UTC on 17 October and 05:30 UTC on 18 October show. Every number
is from a case whose `DONE` reads `complete`, reduced by `warpx/cusp/analyze_picket.py` (ring of coils,
campaign `pw1`) or `analyze_spindle.py` (two coils, campaign `sp2`). Holding times are in electron crossings
unless marked. "Size" is the coil radius in electron orbits, raised by raising the coil current in a device
of fixed physical size; 1× is 78 kA at 0.5× and so on, as in `cusp-results.md`.

## 1. How the ends of the box are treated (0.25×, 4× particles, 400 crossings)

| Box | Ends | Holding time | Electron energy at the end (keV) | Electric field crowding on the axis (1 = none) | Current along the axis ÷ coil current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 period | repeat | 34.2 | 71 | 1.9 | 0.22 |
| 2 periods | repeat | 34.4 | 70 | 1.1 | 0.15 |
| 4 periods | repeat | 41.4 | 45 | 4.1 | 0.47 |
| 1 period | mirrors | 42.4, 42.2 (two seeds) | 43 | 14, 12 | 0.00 |
| 2 periods | mirrors | 38.1, 38.3 (two seeds) | 46 | 180, 183 | 0.02 |
| 4 periods | mirrors | 39.6 | 42 | 39 | 0.08 |
| 1 period, 16× particles | mirrors | 44.1 | 41 | 11 | 0.00 |
| 1 period, mesh twice as fine | mirrors | 42.2 | 44 | 18 | 0.00 |

- The runs fall into two states. One and two repeating periods: electrons stay at 70 keV, 34 crossings.
  Everything else: electrons cool to 42-46 keV, the plasma held per period rises by 10-28%, 38-44 crossings.
- The four-period repeating run is in the cool state without mirrors and without the pile-up on the axis
  (crowding 4). So the cooling is not made by the mirror planes and not by the pile-up. One case; it was
  still gaining 3% per fifth of the run.
- The pile-up on the axis in mirrored runs is not a mesh or particle-noise artefact: a mesh twice as fine
  and 16× the particles leave it where it was, and two seeds agree to 10%. It lowers the holding time by
  about a tenth where it is large (38 against 42).
- Not known: what keeps electrons hot in the one- and two-period repeating boxes. It is not simply the
  current along the axis, because the four-period run carries more of it (0.47) and is cool.

## 3. Larger sizes

- 1×, mesh twice as fine (`pw_s1_h1.0_nr768_ppc1_half`): 101.1 against 103.6 on the standard mesh with the
  same number of particles and length. The mesh is fine enough at 1×.
- 1× still depends on the particle count: 91.2 with 16× the particles against 103.6 with 4×, same length.
- 1× mirrored, standard particles: 112.8, crowding 267. Spoiled by the pile-up. The 4×-particle mirrored
  run hung at 90% (no output for 30 minutes) and is failed.
- 0.375× with 16× the particles (`pw_s0.375_h1.0_ppc21.33`): 49.7 and still gaining 3.7% per fifth, against
  45.2 at 4×. So 0.375× is 45-50, not "45, solid".
- 4× at the low fill (`pw_s4_h0.7`): 307 crossings in a run 300 crossings long, crowding on the axis 4200
  with repeating ends. Not a result: the run is shorter than its own holding time, and the axis field is
  not physical.
- 2× with 4× the particles failed three times at 59% from a runner fault (below). Restarted from 57%.
